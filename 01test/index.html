Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the responsible, safe, and ethical use of digital technologies such as the internet, social media, computers, and mobile devices.

What is Digital Law?

Digital Law refers to the laws and legal rules that govern how people use technology, computers, the internet, and digital information.

What is AUP?

An Acceptable Use Policy is a set of rules that explains how people are allowed to use an organization's technology, internet, computers, and digital resources.

What is Plagerism?

Plagiarism is using someone else's words, ideas, images, or work without giving them proper credit, and presenting it as your own.

In simple terms: Plagiarism is taking someone else's work or ideas and pretending they are yours.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is any form of cheating, lying, or unfair behavior used to gain an advantage in school or academic work.

In simple terms: Academic dishonesty means breaking academic rules to gain an unfair advantage or misrepresent your work.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

Rules for AI-generated content are guidelines about how students can use tools like ChatGPT to help with schoolwork. The exact rules depend on the school, professor, or assignment.
